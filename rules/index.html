Rules & Regulations

Official conduct standards, operational guidelines, and regulations governing all members of Osiris Vanguard.

Official Notice

All members are expected to read, understand, and comply with these regulations. Membership within Osiris Vanguard signifies acceptance of the standards listed below.

01

General Conduct

  • All members must maintain respectful and professional behavior.
  • Harassment, discrimination, threats, and targeted abuse are prohibited.
  • Do not deliberately disrupt community activities or member interactions.
  • Follow the instructions of authorized staff when they are acting within their responsibilities.
  • Members are responsible for their own actions and conduct.
02

Communication Standards

  • Keep discussions relevant to the channel or activity.
  • Spam, excessive mentions, and disruptive message flooding are prohibited.
  • Do not impersonate staff, command personnel, or other members.
  • Use appropriate language and maintain a respectful tone.
  • Resolve disagreements without escalating into personal attacks.
03

Privacy & Security

  • Do not disclose private member information without permission.
  • Sharing private messages, recordings, or personal details without consent may be restricted.
  • Never request or share passwords, authentication codes, or account credentials.
  • Report suspicious links, compromised accounts, and security concerns to staff.
  • Do not attempt to access accounts, channels, or systems without authorization.
04

Facility & Community Rules

  • Follow the rules of the relevant game, server, and community environment.
  • Do not exploit glitches or use unauthorized tools to gain an unfair advantage.
  • Respect designated restricted areas and roleplay boundaries.
  • Do not intentionally interfere with another member's roleplay or event.
  • Where Facility RP is active, remain within the established scenario and its rules.
05

Ranks & Authority

  • Rank does not grant permission to harass, threaten, or mistreat members.
  • Authority must be exercised responsibly and within assigned duties.
  • Do not falsely claim a rank, title, or command position.
  • Members may raise concerns about staff decisions through the appropriate channels.
  • Abuse of authority may result in disciplinary action.
06

Applications & Recruitment

  • Applications must contain accurate and honest information.
  • Do not copy another member's application or submit fabricated credentials.
  • Repeatedly pressuring reviewers for a decision is prohibited.
  • Recruitment must follow the currently established process.
  • Staff may reject applications that fail to meet the stated requirements.
07

Events & Operations

  • Follow the event briefing and instructions provided by authorized organizers.
  • Do not disrupt active events or intentionally sabotage team activities.
  • Respect event attendance, preparation, and conduct requirements.
  • Do not claim participation, achievements, or results that did not occur.
  • Operational details must remain within the authorized audience when marked private.
08

Voice Channel Regulations

  • Keep microphone use appropriate and avoid excessive background noise.
  • Do not interrupt briefings or deliberately talk over other members.
  • Soundboards, music, and audio effects must not disrupt communication.
  • Follow channel-specific requirements and staff instructions.
  • Do not record or distribute private voice conversations without appropriate permission.
09

Advertising & External Content

  • Unauthorized advertising, recruitment, or promotional links are prohibited.
  • Do not distribute malicious links, suspicious downloads, or harmful content.
  • External content must comply with the community's standards.
  • Use designated channels for approved promotions.
10

Reports & Evidence

  • Reports must be submitted honestly and in good faith.
  • Provide relevant context and evidence whenever possible.
  • Do not fabricate, alter, or deliberately misrepresent evidence.
  • False or malicious reports may result in disciplinary action.
  • Do not publicly expose private reports or ongoing moderation cases without authorization.
11

Staff & Moderation Conduct

  • Staff must act fairly, consistently, and professionally.
  • Moderation tools must only be used for legitimate administrative purposes.
  • Staff must not disclose confidential moderation information without authorization.
  • Personal disputes must not influence moderation decisions.
  • Abuse or misuse of staff permissions may result in removal from the position.
12

Partnerships & Affiliations

  • Partnerships must be approved by authorized representatives.
  • Members must not make unofficial commitments on behalf of Osiris Vanguard.
  • Do not misrepresent the status or terms of an affiliation.
  • Partnership-related disputes should be directed to the appropriate leadership.
13

Account Integrity & Rule Evasion

  • Do not use alternate accounts to evade moderation or restrictions.
  • Do not exploit account changes to conceal misconduct.
  • Impersonation and fraudulent identity claims are prohibited.
  • Members must comply with applicable platform rules and terms of service.
14

Enforcement & Disciplinary Action

Violations may result in action depending on severity, context, frequency, and available evidence.

  • Verbal or written warning.
  • Temporary restriction or removal from an activity.
  • Suspension or removal from the community.
  • Removal of rank, role, or privileges.
  • Permanent restriction for severe or repeated violations.

Osiris Vanguard reserves the right to determine an appropriate response to conduct that threatens the safety or integrity of the community.

15

Appeals & Review

  • Members may submit an appeal through the designated process.
  • Appeals should include relevant context and supporting evidence.
  • Do not spam staff or repeatedly submit identical appeals.
  • Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that a decision will be reversed.
  • Abusive or deliberately false appeals may be dismissed.
16

Final Notice

These regulations exist to maintain a disciplined, respectful, and organized community.

All members are expected to remain informed of applicable rules and updates. Ignorance of the regulations does not automatically exempt a member from accountability.
