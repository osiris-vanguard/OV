Community Rules

Official rules and regulations for the Osiris Vanguard community.

ONE UNIT. ONE MISSION.
By joining or participating in Osiris Vanguard, you agree to follow these rules. These guidelines apply to all members, regardless of rank, role, membership status, or platform.

01. General Conduct

  • Treat all members with respect and maintain a professional attitude.
  • Harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate speech, and targeted abuse are prohibited.
  • Do not provoke unnecessary arguments or deliberately disrupt the community.
  • Respect the decisions of staff members and use the appropriate channels for disputes.
  • Do not encourage others to violate community rules.
  • Repeated disruptive behavior may result in moderation action.

02. Discord Communication

  • Use channels for their intended purposes.
  • Spam, excessive mentions, message flooding, and disruptive behavior are prohibited.
  • Do not impersonate members, staff, or other individuals.
  • Do not post malicious links, suspicious files, or unauthorized advertisements.
  • Do not bypass channel restrictions or moderation actions.
  • Keep conversations appropriate for the community.
  • Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

03. Privacy & Security

  • Do not share another person's private or personal information without permission.
  • Sharing private messages, staff discussions, or restricted information without authorization is prohibited.
  • Never request or share passwords, account credentials, or authentication codes.
  • Do not distribute malicious software, phishing links, or suspicious downloads.
  • Report security concerns or suspicious activity to staff.
  • Do not attempt to access accounts, channels, or systems without authorization.

04. Facility Roleplay

  • Follow the rules of the Roblox experience and any official event guidelines.
  • Exploiting, cheating, scripting, or using unauthorized third-party tools is prohibited.
  • Do not abuse glitches, bugs, or unintended game mechanics for an unfair advantage.
  • Do not intentionally disrupt roleplay sessions, events, or other players' experiences.
  • Follow faction procedures and instructions given by authorized leaders during official activities.
  • Do not engage in unauthorized team-killing, spawn-killing, or intentional team disruption.
  • Do not abuse game mechanics to avoid legitimate consequences.
  • Report serious bugs or exploits to the appropriate staff members.

05. Rank, Authority & Leadership

  • Ranks and permissions must be used only for their intended purposes.
  • Do not abuse authority to threaten, harass, or unfairly punish members.
  • Do not claim a rank, position, or permission that you have not been granted.
  • Do not interfere with another authorized leader's responsibilities without a valid reason.
  • Leadership decisions should be made fairly and in accordance with community standards.
  • Report suspected rank or permission abuse through the appropriate channels.

06. Applications & Membership

  • Provide accurate and honest information in applications.
  • Do not plagiarize, falsify credentials, or misrepresent your experience.
  • Do not pressure staff to approve applications or bypass the review process.
  • Do not submit repeated applications to avoid a pending review or rejection.
  • Membership, rank, and access may be reviewed if a member violates community standards.
  • Application decisions must be respected. Appeals may be submitted through designated channels.

07. Events & Operations

  • Follow the instructions provided by event hosts and authorized leaders.
  • Arrive on time when attendance is required.
  • Do not disrupt briefings, formations, training sessions, or official operations.
  • Do not falsely claim attendance or completion of an event.
  • Do not share restricted operation details outside authorized channels.
  • Follow event-specific rules in addition to these community rules.
  • Event hosts may remove participants who repeatedly disrupt the activity.

08. Voice Channels

  • Do not use disruptive audio, soundboards, or microphone spam.
  • Do not interrupt official briefings, meetings, or events.
  • Respect other members and follow channel-specific instructions.
  • Do not record or distribute private conversations without appropriate permission.
  • Keep communication clear and relevant during official activities.

09. Advertising & External Content

  • Unauthorized advertising, recruitment, or promotion is prohibited.
  • Do not promote competing communities in restricted channels.
  • External links must be safe, relevant, and permitted by the server rules.
  • Do not distribute stolen content or content that violates another person's rights.
  • Obtain staff approval before organizing official partnerships or promotional activities.

10. Reports & Evidence

  • Submit reports through the designated reporting channels.
  • Provide accurate information and relevant evidence when reporting a violation.
  • Do not fabricate, manipulate, or knowingly misrepresent evidence.
  • Do not use reports to harass, threaten, or target another member.
  • False or malicious reports may result in moderation action.
  • Do not publicly expose private investigations or restricted staff discussions.

11. Staff & Moderation

  • Follow reasonable instructions from authorized staff members.
  • Do not impersonate staff or claim authority you do not have.
  • Do not interfere with moderation actions or encourage others to do so.
  • If you disagree with a moderation decision, submit a respectful appeal through the designated channel.
  • Staff members are expected to act fairly and responsibly.
  • Staff permissions must not be used for personal disputes or retaliation.
  • Moderation discussions should remain confidential when designated as private.

12. Partnerships & Representation

  • Only authorized representatives may make official statements on behalf of Osiris Vanguard.
  • Do not promise partnerships, ranks, or benefits without proper authorization.
  • Do not misrepresent the community's position, leadership, or policies.
  • Official announcements must be shared through approved channels.

13. Rule Evasion & Alternate Accounts

  • Do not use alternate accounts to evade bans, timeouts, or other moderation actions.
  • Do not assist another member in bypassing a restriction.
  • Do not use multiple accounts to manipulate votes, applications, events, or community activity.
  • Staff may review suspected evasion when supported by relevant information.

14. Enforcement

Violations may result in moderation action depending on their severity, frequency, intent, and impact on the community.

  • Verbal or written warning
  • Message removal
  • Timeout or temporary restriction
  • Removal of roles or membership privileges
  • Removal from an event or operation
  • Temporary suspension or permanent removal from the community

Serious violations may result in immediate action without a prior warning. Staff may consider the circumstances of each incident.

15. Appeals

  • Members may appeal moderation actions through designated channels.
  • Appeals must be respectful, relevant, and supported by accurate information.
  • Do not spam staff or repeatedly submit the same appeal while it is under review.
  • Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that a moderation action will be reversed.
  • Staff decisions may be reviewed when new relevant evidence is provided.

16. Final Notice

These rules are intended to maintain a safe, organized, and respectful environment for all members. Osiris Vanguard reserves the right to update these guidelines when necessary.

Members are responsible for staying informed about the current rules. Continued participation in the community indicates acceptance of these guidelines.
