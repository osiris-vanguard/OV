Community Rules

Official rules and guidelines for the Osiris Vanguard community.

ONE UNIT. ONE MISSION.
By joining or participating in our community, you agree to follow these rules. These guidelines apply to all members, regardless of rank, role, or membership status.

01. General Conduct

  • Treat all members with respect and maintain a professional attitude.
  • Harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate speech, and targeted abuse are prohibited.
  • Do not provoke unnecessary arguments or deliberately disrupt the community.
  • Respect the decisions of staff members and use the appropriate channels for disputes.

02. Discord Communication

  • Use channels for their intended purposes.
  • Spam, excessive mentions, message flooding, and disruptive behavior are prohibited.
  • Do not impersonate members, staff, or other individuals.
  • Do not post malicious links, suspicious files, or unauthorized advertisements.
  • Keep conversations appropriate for the community and follow Discord's Terms of Service.

03. Privacy & Security

  • Do not share another person's private or personal information without permission.
  • Sharing private messages, staff discussions, or restricted information without authorization is prohibited.
  • Never request or share passwords, account credentials, or authentication codes.
  • Report security concerns or suspicious activity to staff.

04. Facility Roleplay

  • Follow the rules of the Roblox experience and any official event guidelines.
  • Exploiting, cheating, scripting, or using unauthorized third-party tools is prohibited.
  • Do not abuse glitches, bugs, or unintended game mechanics for an unfair advantage.
  • Do not intentionally disrupt roleplay sessions, events, or other players' experiences.
  • Follow faction procedures and instructions given by authorized leaders during official activities.

05. Applications & Membership

  • Provide accurate and honest information in applications.
  • Do not plagiarize, falsify credentials, or misrepresent your experience.
  • Do not pressure staff to approve applications or bypass the review process.
  • Membership, rank, and access may be reviewed if a member violates community standards.

06. Staff & Moderation

  • Follow reasonable instructions from authorized staff members.
  • Do not impersonate staff or claim authority you do not have.
  • Do not interfere with moderation actions or encourage others to do so.
  • If you disagree with a moderation decision, submit a respectful appeal through the designated channel.
  • Staff members are expected to act fairly and responsibly.
