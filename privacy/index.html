1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Osiris Vanguard Bot (“the Bot”) may collect, use, store, and process information when it is used on Discord.

The Bot is designed to support the management and operation of the Osiris Vanguard faction within Facility Roleplay. We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide the Bot’s features.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled and how the Bot is configured, the following information may be processed:

  • Discord Account Information: Discord User ID, username or display name, Discord Server (Guild) ID, and role or membership information.
  • Application and Verification Information: information submitted through application commands or forms, Roblox username if requested, preferred role or position, previous experience if provided, timezone if provided, additional voluntarily submitted details, application status, and staff review information.
  • Server Configuration Information: server ID, channel IDs, role IDs, Bot configuration settings, and verification or application system settings.
  • Operational Information: Bot commands and interactions, administrative actions performed through the Bot, error reports and diagnostic information, and logs necessary for security, troubleshooting, or moderation where enabled.

The exact information processed depends on the features enabled in the server and the Bot’s actual implementation.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing server management and automation features.
  • Processing verification requests and faction applications.
  • Assigning roles and managing authorized access.
  • Supporting administrative review and moderation.
  • Maintaining server configuration and functionality.
  • Detecting abuse, misuse, or security issues.
  • Troubleshooting errors and improving reliability.

We do not intentionally use Discord API data for purposes unrelated to the Bot’s functionality.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in the Bot’s database, configuration files, or hosting environment, depending on the implementation.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law.

Application records, configuration data, and operational logs may have different retention periods.

When information is no longer necessary, we may delete or anonymize it, subject to technical limitations and applicable legal requirements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed or shared only when reasonably necessary:

  • To provide the Bot’s features.
  • With authorized Osiris Vanguard administrators or staff for application review, verification, moderation, and server management.
  • With hosting or infrastructure providers that operate the Bot.
  • To comply with applicable laws or valid legal requests.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the Bot or its users.

Third-party services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Discord and Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord’s platform and APIs. Discord may independently collect and process information according to its own policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the Bot.

These measures may include access restrictions, permission controls, and safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no system can guarantee absolute security. Users and server administrators should avoid sharing sensitive personal information through the Bot unless it is necessary for the intended functionality.

8. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request the deletion of information associated with their Discord account.

To request deletion, contact the Osiris Vanguard administration team through the official Discord server .

Please include your Discord User ID and a description of the information you would like deleted.

We may need to retain certain information where required by applicable law, for security purposes, or to resolve outstanding issues.

Where technically possible and legally appropriate, eligible information will be deleted or anonymized after the request is reviewed.

Removing the Bot from a server does not necessarily delete information previously stored by the Bot.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Bot is intended for users who meet Discord’s minimum age requirements and any applicable age requirements in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals who are not permitted to use Discord under the applicable age requirements.

If you believe information has been collected from an ineligible user, please contact the administration team.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot’s features, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be made available through this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the Osiris Vanguard administration team through the official Discord server .
